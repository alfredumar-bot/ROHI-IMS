ROHI Attendance Geofence Update

All configured ROHI State Offices now use a strict 50 metre geofence for both Check-In and Check-Out.

Updated offices:
- Borno State Office: 50 m
- Adamawa HQ: 50 m
- Yobe State Office: 50 m
- Taraba State Office: 50 m
- Benue State Office: 50 m
- Sokoto State Office: 50 m

The fallback/default geofence radius is also 50 m.

A GPS coordinate is accepted only when the captured phone location is at or below 50 metres from the fixed office coordinate.
